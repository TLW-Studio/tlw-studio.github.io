Paad Race Privacy Policy

Effective 27 November 2026

Paad Race is a game by TLW Studio, an independent developer in India. It is made for families, including children. This page explains, in plain words, what information the game uses and why.

The short version

  • There are no accounts and no sign-in. The game never asks for your real name, email, phone number, photos, contacts or location.
  • Your progress, coins and settings are saved only on your phone.
  • We use Google's Firebase to learn how the game is played and to fix crashes. This uses an app ID, not who you are.
  • Ads appear only if you choose to watch one for a reward. They are set up for children: not personalised, and no advertising ID is collected.
  • Purchases go through Google Play. We never see card or payment details, and a grown-up check comes before every purchase.
  • We do not sell any information.

Information saved on your phone

The game saves your progress, stars, coins, floats, upgrades, settings and the player name you type in, on your phone only. We do not receive it. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes it.

Playing with friends on the same Wi-Fi

When you make or join a room, phones on the same Wi-Fi network talk to each other directly. They share the player name you chose, your rider's look and colour, the float, and what happens in the race, so everyone sees the same game. This stays on your local network and does not pass through our servers. There is no chat. We suggest children use a nickname, not their real name.

Game statistics and crash reports (Firebase)

To make the game better, we use Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, run by Google. They collect:

  • How the game is played: for example, which river was started or finished, the time and stars, which float was chosen, how far an endless run went, and whether a shop item or reward ad was opened.
  • Crash reports: what the game was doing when it crashed, the app version, the phone model and the Android version.
  • Basic device information, such as phone model, Android version, language and an approximate country, and a random app installation ID made by Firebase.

This information is not linked to your name or any account. Advertising ID collection, ad personalisation and the sharing of analytics for advertising are switched off. Google processes this information for us as a service provider. Statistics are kept only as long as our Firebase settings allow, and then deleted automatically.

Reward ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows an ad only when a player taps a button to watch one for a reward, such as a patch for the float or double coins. There are no pop-up ads between rounds. Every ad request is marked as directed to children and under the age of consent, asks only for general audience (G-rated) ads, and asks for non-personalised ads. The game does not collect the advertising ID. To show an ad and prevent fraud, Google AdMob may process information such as the device's IP address, model and operating system. Buying "No ads" removes the ads.

Purchases (Google Play)

Coins, the Starter pack and "No ads" are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, so we never see card, UPI or bank details. The game receives only the order number and whether it was paid, so it can give the item once. A grown-up check (a sum to solve) comes before every purchase.

Children

Paad Race is designed to be safe for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game has no accounts, chat or links to social media, and the privacy policy link inside the game is behind a grown-up check. If you are a parent and have a question or concern, please email us.

Your choices

  • Clearing the game's data or uninstalling it deletes everything saved on the phone and resets the Firebase installation ID.
  • You can ask us to delete any statistics linked to your app installation by emailing us. Because we do not know who you are, please include the date and phone model so we can find them.

Security

Information sent to Google's services travels over encrypted connections.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. Big changes will also be mentioned in the game's update notes.
